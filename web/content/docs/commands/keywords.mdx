---
title: Keyword research
description: Look up Bing search demand for any query and market with bwt keyword research commands.
---

Keyword research reports Bing search demand for a query in a market. It is market data, not your
site's performance, so it needs no `--site`.

```bash
bwt keywords get "running shoes"                              # impressions over a date range
bwt keywords stats "running shoes"                            # weekly impression history
bwt keywords related "running shoes" --country gb --language en-GB
```

`--country` (default `us`) and `--language` (default `en-US`) pick the market. `get` and `related`
take `--start` and `--end` dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`); `--end` defaults to today (UTC) and `--start` to 30
days before it. Microsoft does not document the accepted date syntax, so `bwt` sends `YYYY-MM-DD`,
which works in live checks.
